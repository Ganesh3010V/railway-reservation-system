Cancel Ticket

Stored Procedure: CancelBooking + Trigger: trg_free_seat_after_cancel

Cancelling a confirmed seat triggers trg_free_seat_after_cancel (frees the Seat row), then the CancelBooking stored procedure finds the earliest waitlisted passenger with an overlapping segment and auto-promotes them to Confirmed.

Generate Bill

Stored Procedure: GenerateBill (fare = distance × FarePerKm)

The GenerateBill stored procedure computes fare by looking up DistanceFromOrigin for both boarding and exit stations via RouteStation, multiplies the difference by FarePerKm from SeatClass, and inserts a Payment record.

This chart works exactly like the Indian Railway Reservation Chart — it shows seat-wise allocation with passenger names, a donut chart for booking status breakdown, and the waitlist queue. It auto-updates after every booking or cancellation.
